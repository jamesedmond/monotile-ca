Single gliders

seventeen gliders under four rules

The discovery paper found a glider under each of four rules. The census launched thousands of small seeds under the same rules and certified the single gliders that settled out of them. There are seventeen.

Most of them are hard to tell apart by eye. Under one rule the gliders have the same few states in similar arrangements, and they travel in the same directions at the same speed. The census tells them apart by their certificates. A certificate is a finite set of situations, each a pattern with the tiles around it, and two gliders are the same object only if their sets of situations coincide.

The number on each tab is the number of situations in the certificate. Each viewer flies the selected glider in a sliding window for as long as it stays on screen. The heading in the corner of a viewer is measured in that record's own frame, so the same lane can show headings that differ by a multiple of 60°.

Hat A's rule

Five gliders. The 381 is the discovery paper's hat A. The 380 is launched twice as often and shares no situation with it. The 257, the 256 and the 127 are close relatives: with four rings of tiles kept around the pattern their certificates run together into one set of 630 situations, and with five rings they separate.

Hat B's rule

The same five gliders in another alphabet. Take any of hat A's five, remove its state-3 cells and rename its other two states, and every situation becomes a situation of the matching glider under hat B's rule, with every transition carried over. The two rules were found by separate searches and have different tables, of eight rows and four.

Hat C's rule

Five gliders, and none of them is one of hat A's in another alphabet: no renaming of states, with or without removing cells, turns a situation of one into a situation of the other.

The spectre rule

Two gliders, on two families of lanes about 9° apart. The discovery paper gave the spectre tiling a single compass offset; that offset holds for its glider, and the second glider has another. The second lane family has its own page, which follows one seed that launches both gliders.
