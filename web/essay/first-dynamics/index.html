The game on the board

the rule space gets its first census, and one rule moves

Nothing in the engine changes for this section: to the engine, a hat patch is an adjacency graph, just as the square grid was. What changes is the texture of the world: neighbour counts now vary tile by tile (five or six for a hat, always four for an anti-hat, four to seven on spectres — counted across shared edges; the vertex flavour returns later), the mix shifting from place to place with no repeating rhythm, and beyond the patch edge every cell is permanently dead. Here is Life's rule, transplanted — the rule only; the neighbourhood is the board's own:

A census of every rule

On a board with at most seven neighbours, the whole space of birth/survival rules is small enough to enumerate: 8,192 rules on the hat, 32,768 on the spectre (excluding birth-on-zero, which sets the infinite vacuum strobing — behaviour a dead-boundary patch cannot model). Running all of them — three random soups each, 1,024 generations, every outcome classified — takes under three seconds on a laptop. It sorts as follows:

The census's texture matches the square-grid intuition in one way: the rules still active after a thousand generations cluster in the Life-adjacent corner, births on roughly half the neighbourhood. It also poses a shape question. The patch is a graph-distance ball and looks hexagonal — so what shape does growth take?

The bookmarks

Inside the oscillating buckets are some spectacular specimens. A whole-patch soup on the hat recurs with global period 318 — and 318 = 2·3·53, so somewhere in that patch a local oscillator with period divisible by 53 is ticking. One early rule earned a permanent bookmark by doing something no oscillator does: growing with a direction. On the spectre tiling, B2/S256 — ignited by one specific four-cell seed at one specific anchor class; of ten classes and two seed shapes tried, exactly that combination — grows a churning, quasi-one-dimensional streak that holds a single compass bearing for thousands of generations. Directed structure, on a board with no translational symmetry to point the way.
