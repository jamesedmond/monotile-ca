Code and records

The toolchain, the search drivers, every committed result record, and the sources of this site are public at github.com/jamesedmond/monotile-ca, under the MIT and Apache-2.0 licences. Every panel in the essay carries a ⤓ button that downloads the exact record it is replaying and shows the one command that re-verifies it natively; the Playground page gives clone-and-run instructions. Cellular automata on aperiodic tilings

  • N. Owens, S. Stepney, "Investigations of Game of Life Cellular Automata Rules on Penrose Tilings: Lifetime, Ash, and Oscillator Statistics," Journal of Cellular Automata 5(3), 2010.

  • Ready (GollyGang), reaction-diffusion and cellular-automata simulator. github.com/GollyGang/ready — carries the reference kernel for Goucher's rule.

Acknowledgements

The boards exist because of Simon Tatham's finite-state transducers for substitution tilings, which give every tile a name and answer adjacency from names alone. The work was carried out with substantial assistance from an AI coding agent (Claude, Anthropic), which implemented the toolchain and searches and drafted analyses under the author's direction; every claim was verified by structural controls — pre-stated kill-conditions, cross-radius and causality checks — and by the author's independent review of the replayable records.

Colophon

One engine, two targets: the cellular-automaton engine is a no_std Rust crate compiled natively for the searches and to WebAssembly for these pages, so what you watch is the code that produced the results, not an illustration of it. Patches are regenerated in your browser from their addresses; geometry is drawn with WebGL2; the pages are static files built with Vite and served from Cloudflare Pages. There are no analytics and no cookies; nothing you do here leaves your machine.
