Write your own rule

The table-rule editor accepts the notation this essay has used throughout — one priority row per line, first match wins, no match means ground:

you writeit means
states 4k = 4 states (0 = ground)
2 | n2>=1 -> 0a state-2 cell with a state-2 neighbour becomes ground
* | n3>=1 & n2>=1 -> 1any cell seeing state 3 and state 2 fires
* | otherwise -> 3a wildcard row with no conditions

The editor opens prefilled with hat glider A's rule, and one button copies any loaded record's table into it. The suggested first experiment is the ablation in miniature: load hat A, break one row, and watch what a glider is made of. The suggested second is the one nobody has run: find something we missed. Vertex adjacency, four states, a two-cell seed — the space that took this project three levers and a control experiment to reach is now one dropdown away.

Reports of anything interesting travel fastest as a downloaded record. Good hunting.
