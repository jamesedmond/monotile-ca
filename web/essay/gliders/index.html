Four verified gliders, five lanes — hat C's launch emits three gliders on two headings, and the spectre's seed emits a twin pair — and each was flown under the sliding-window instrument to one million path-rings (≈880,000 effective rings from launch), at a constant ~112 MB, on a laptop. Every viewer below is that instrument running live: the gliders fly for as long as you keep them on screen, and they are going somewhere no simulation has been.

On immortality, the honest ledger: the only late transition ever observed remains the looper's capture at ring 189. Each of these flights has put roughly 4,600× that distance behind it with max population flat the whole way. Not a proof — the certificate section is about what proof would take — but the empirical margin is now three and a half orders of magnitude.

Each entry: the glider in flight, the rule it obeys — rendered directly from the committed record, dead rows and all, exactly as the GA evolved it (first matching row applies; no match means ground) — and its worldtube in space-time.
